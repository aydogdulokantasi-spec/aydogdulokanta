Good to Know

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 07:00–17:00. Closed on Sundays. Soups are ready from 07:00.

How does it work?

It is a traditional Turkish esnaf lokantası: the dishes of the day are on display at the counter. Point to what you would like; you can order a full or half portion.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit cards and Turkish meal cards (Multinet, Pluxee, Ticket Restaurant, Setcard, Metropol).

Is the meat halal? Do you serve alcohol?

Yes, all our meat is halal. We do not serve alcohol.

When is it least busy?

Lunch is busiest 12:00–13:30. For no queue, come 11:00–12:00 or 13:30–15:00.

Where exactly are you?

Inside Bostancı Oto Sanayi (industrial estate): Huzur Hoca Caddesi No:76/A, İçerenköy, Ataşehir, Istanbul. Use the map above for directions.

Aydoğdu Lokantası has served traditional Turkish food in Bostancı Oto Sanayi, Ataşehir since 1987: stews and oven dishes, döner, regional dishes and friendly service.
